We make apps that help people follow through.

Stonefield designs apps for phones and the web. Spikie helps you keep your goals for the week; Tagwerk helps you lift, eat well and keep good daily habits.

Spikie

You pick your goals for the next 7 days and put your own money on each one. Keep your word and you pay nothing. Miss a goal and you pay the amount you set, and we give half of it to a charity you choose.

You can't win anything in Spikie. For iPhone and Android, adults only, in a private beta.

Tagwerk

Your healthy life, in one app. Tagwerk plans your training, food and daily habits, tracks them and coaches you: it sets your next weight and pace from what you did.

The first 7 days are free, then €10 a week or €100 a year, through the App Store. iPhone only. It's not medical advice.

Spikie: prices and payments

  • The app is free. No subscription, no account fee, nothing paid up front.
  • You only pay for a goal you miss, exactly the amount you set for it (PLN 10 to 1,000 per goal). A goal with no answer after the week counts as missed.
  • We charge the payment method saved in the app, no earlier than 12 hours after the week is settled. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number.
  • Amounts are in PLN and include VAT where it applies. On request, we issue an invoice or another document confirming a charge.
  • Once a month we give half of every charge to the charity you picked, as our own donation. The other half is our income.

Spikie: disputes, complaints and refunds

  • Disputing a charge. Within 7 days of the week's settlement you can dispute a charge in the app. We decide within 48 hours. If we agree with you, we cancel the charge or refund it to the same payment method.
  • Complaints. Email hello@stonefield.app. We reply within 14 days.
  • Withdrawal. You can withdraw from the contract within 14 days of creating your account, without giving a reason. A running week then ends with no charge.

The full rules are in the Spikie Terms (§ 6–10). Tagwerk's subscriptions, cancellations and refunds go through the App Store; its terms are on tagwerk.stonefield.app.
