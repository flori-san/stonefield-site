Tagwerk

Your healthy life, in one app. Lift, eat well and keep good daily habits. Tagwerk plans it, tracks it and coaches you: a coach that sets your next weight and pace from what you did.

iPhone · English

The first 7 days are free, with no account needed and nothing to cancel — the free week never turns into a paid subscription by itself. After that, €10 a week or €100 a year, through the App Store.

Tagwerk is a tracker, not medical advice. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything and doesn't replace a doctor, a dietitian or a physiotherapist.
