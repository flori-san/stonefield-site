Plushie

Weekly goals. For real. Set up to 20 yes/no goals for the week and stake money on each one. Keep them all and you pay nothing; miss one and you pay the amount you chose — half of it goes to a charity you pick.

iPhone, Android · Private beta in Poland; coming to the App Store and Google Play.

In Plushie you can't win anything: you can only keep your money or pay for a goal you didn't meet. The app is free, and a charge happens only when you yourself report that a goal didn't work out. Adults only.
