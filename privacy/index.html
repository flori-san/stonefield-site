Privacy policy

Effective from 25 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how Stonefield Sp. z o.o. handles personal data on this website (stonefield.app) and, unless an app says otherwise, in our apps.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Stonefield Sp. z o.o., ul. Śniadeckich 10C/33, 60-774 Poznań, Poland (KRS 0001277274, NIP 7792614016, REGON 546122016). You can reach us about data protection at hello@stonefield.app.

2. This website

stonefield.app is a static website. It has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. We do not collect or store any personal data when you browse it, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you load a page, GitHub's servers receive the technical data every website receives — your IP address, browser type and the page requested — in their server logs, which GitHub keeps and uses for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement. That transfer to the USA is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

3. If you write to us

If you email hello@stonefield.app, we receive your email address, name (if you include it) and the content of your message. We use them only to answer you and to keep the correspondence, through Google (Gmail), which hosts our mailbox (USA). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR — answering your request and our legitimate interest in keeping a record of communication. We delete correspondence after 24 months unless a matter is still open or the law requires longer.

4. Our apps

Each app has its own privacy policy that describes the data the app itself collects:

  • Spikie: spikie.stonefield.app/en/privacy/
  • Tagwerk: tagwerk.stonefield.app/privacy.html
  • Keyline (in testing): handled under this policy until its own pages are published.

5. Why we use data, and on what legal basis

Only for what you see: serving this website, answering your emails, and running and improving our apps. We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with anyone except the processors listed above (hosting, email, and the app backends described in each app's policy), each bound by a data processing agreement, and authorities where the law requires.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Hosting this website involves GitHub, Inc. in the USA (see section 2). Our app backends keep data in the EU. Any other transfer happens only with an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep data

Only as long as needed for the purpose: server logs are kept by GitHub per its own policy; email correspondence for 24 months; app data as described in each app's privacy policy, and deleted when you delete your account in the app.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to: give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, or with the authority in your country of residence. Write to hello@stonefield.app and we will respond within one month.

9. Security

We protect data with the measures appropriate to the risk: encrypted connections (HTTPS) everywhere, access limited to what each person needs, and processors bound by contracts. To report a security issue, see our security.txt.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when something changes — a new app, a new provider, a change in the law. The current version is always on this page with its date. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app or by email where we can.
